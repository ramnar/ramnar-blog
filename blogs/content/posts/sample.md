This is a sample post. Replace it with a thought, a project update, or something you have learned recently.

## Start with one idea

A good post does not need to cover everything. Pick one idea, explain why it matters, and give the reader enough context to follow along.

> Clear writing starts with knowing what you want to say.

You can emphasize a phrase with **bold text**, add a little nuance with *italics*, or mention a command such as `hugo server` inline.

## A short checklist

- Give the post a specific title.
- Break longer thoughts into sections.
- Add links when they help the reader explore further.

For example, visit the [Hugo documentation](https://gohugo.io/documentation/) to learn more about building your site.
