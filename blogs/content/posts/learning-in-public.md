When learning something new, it is tempting to wait until you understand every detail before writing about it. That can mean never writing anything down at all.

Small notes are a better place to start. Capture the question you had, what you tried, and what finally made sense. The explanation can be imperfect; you can improve it the next time you return to the subject.

## A simple note-taking loop

1. Write down one question you want to answer.
2. Try a small example and record what happened.
3. Summarize the useful part in your own words.

Over time, these notes become a record of your progress and a practical reference for your future self. Sharing them can help someone else who is asking the same question.
